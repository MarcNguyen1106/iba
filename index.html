My dearest Boba,

Happy 20/10. Today the whole country celebrates its women, and I get to celebrate the one who means the most to me.

I wanted to give you something you could keep, so I made you this little corner of the internet and filled it with pink and white tulips. Pink for how happy you make me. White for how calm and safe I feel when I’m with you.

Thank you for your kindness, your patience and your laugh, and for all the small ways you take care of the people around you, me included. You make my life softer and sweeter, like the last sip of a brown sugar milk tea, the one with all the pearls.

I hope today is as lovely as you are. I’ll keep trying to make every ordinary day feel a little like this one.

Yours, always ♡

      Four true things about tulips, and you

      • They keep growing after they’re cut.

        A tulip in a vase can stretch another two or three centimetres. My love for you does the same, a little more every day.

      • They turn toward the light.

        Leave tulips by a window and they’ll lean toward it. I keep catching myself leaning toward you.

      • They open in the morning and close at night.

        Tulip petals respond to warmth and light. My mornings open the same way when you’re in them.

      • They were once worth a house.

        During the Dutch tulip craze of the 1630s, a single rare bulb could sell for the price of a house in Amsterdam. You’re the rarer find.
